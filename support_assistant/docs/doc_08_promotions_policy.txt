Zepto Promotional Offers and Coupon Policy

Zepto regularly offers promotional discounts, cashback offers, and referral bonuses. Coupons and promo codes can be applied at checkout in the designated promo code field. Each coupon has specific terms including minimum order value, eligible product categories, maximum discount cap, and expiry date, all of which are displayed in the offer details. Only one coupon code can be applied per order; stacking multiple coupons is not permitted. Cashback offers are credited to the Zepto Wallet within 24 to 48 hours after the order is delivered and not subject to a return. Referral bonuses are issued to both the referrer and the new user after the referred user completes their first order of a minimum value as specified in the referral program terms. Promotional credits are non-transferable, have no cash value, and expire as specified in the offer terms. Zepto reserves the right to withdraw or modify any promotional offer at any time without prior notice. Misuse of promotional offers, including creating multiple accounts to claim referral bonuses, will result in account suspension and forfeiture of all credits. Orders that use a promotional coupon and are subsequently cancelled or refunded will have the coupon value deducted from the refund amount.
