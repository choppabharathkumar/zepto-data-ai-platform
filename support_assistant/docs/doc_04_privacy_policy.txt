Zepto Privacy Policy

Zepto collects personal information including name, phone number, email address, delivery address, and payment details to process orders and provide services. Location data is collected during active app usage to enable real-time delivery tracking and to verify the serviceability of the customer's address. Zepto does not sell or rent personal data to third parties for marketing purposes. Data may be shared with trusted third-party service providers such as payment processors and logistics partners solely for the purpose of fulfilling orders. Customers may request deletion of their account and associated data by contacting support; deletion requests are processed within 30 days and are subject to legal retention requirements. Zepto uses cookies and analytics tools on its app and website to improve user experience and personalise product recommendations; customers can manage cookie preferences in app settings. Zepto employs industry-standard encryption (TLS) for data in transit and AES-256 for data at rest. In the event of a data breach that may affect customer information, Zepto will notify affected users within 72 hours as per applicable data protection regulations. Customers have the right to access, correct, or object to the processing of their personal data by submitting a request through the app. Zepto retains transaction data for a minimum of 7 years for legal and financial compliance purposes.
