Zepto Product Quality and Freshness Policy

Zepto is committed to delivering fresh, high-quality products to every customer. All perishable products including fruits, vegetables, dairy, meat, and bakery items are sourced daily from verified suppliers and stored in temperature-controlled dark stores. Products are displayed with their expiry or best-before date on both the app and the physical packaging. Zepto does not stock products that are within 48 hours of their expiry date for ambient products, or within 24 hours for refrigerated items. If a customer receives a product that is expired, visibly damaged, or below acceptable quality standards, they are entitled to a full refund or replacement as per the Refund Policy. Zepto conducts random quality audits at its dark stores on a weekly basis and maintains supplier scorecards. Customers can report product quality issues through the app and attach photos; these reports are used to improve supplier accountability. Organic and natural products on the Zepto platform are verified against third-party certifications before being listed. Zepto does not guarantee specific brands or varieties for fresh produce, as substitutions may occur based on stock availability; customers will be notified before substitution. Cold-chain products are handled with insulated packaging to maintain temperature integrity during the last-mile delivery.
