Zepto Delivery Policy

Zepto guarantees delivery within 10 minutes for orders placed within the serviceable area. Orders placed after 11:45 PM may be scheduled for the next available slot starting at 6:00 AM. Delivery is available seven days a week including public holidays. In the event of severe weather, natural disasters, or government-imposed restrictions, Zepto reserves the right to suspend or delay deliveries and will notify affected customers via the app and SMS. Customers living in high-rise buildings must ensure the delivery agent can access the building lobby; Zepto is not responsible for delays caused by restricted building access. A delivery fee may apply for orders below the minimum order value, as specified in the app at checkout. Zepto does not support scheduled future deliveries; all orders are dispatched immediately upon confirmation. Delivery agents are not permitted to enter customer premises; all handovers occur at the door or building entrance. Customers can track their order in real time through the Zepto app once the order is confirmed. If a delivery is delayed beyond 20 minutes, the customer is eligible for a delay compensation credit as per the current promotional policy.
