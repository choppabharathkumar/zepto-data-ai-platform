Zepto Customer Support Policy

Zepto provides customer support through the in-app chat, email, and phone. In-app chat is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Phone support is available from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM IST daily. Email support responses are provided within 24 business hours. All support interactions are logged and assigned a unique ticket number communicated to the customer via app notification or SMS. Priority support is provided to ZeptoPass members, with a guaranteed first response within 5 minutes via in-app chat. Customers should have their order ID, registered phone number, and a brief description of the issue ready when contacting support. Zepto aims to resolve all order-related complaints within 24 hours and complex billing disputes within 5 business days. Escalations that remain unresolved after 7 business days are automatically reviewed by the senior support team. Abusive behaviour toward Zepto support staff will result in account suspension. Customer satisfaction surveys are sent after each support interaction; feedback is used for agent training and process improvement. Zepto does not offer support for issues related to third-party apps or devices used to access the Zepto platform.
