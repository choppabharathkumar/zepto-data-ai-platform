Zepto Payment Policy

Zepto accepts the following payment methods: UPI, credit cards, debit cards, net banking, Zepto Wallet, and Cash on Delivery (COD). COD is available on orders up to Rs. 2,000 and is subject to availability in the customer's delivery area. Zepto Wallet can be loaded using any supported payment method and the balance does not expire. Payments are processed securely through PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways. In the event of a payment failure at checkout, the customer's account will not be charged; any temporary hold placed by the bank will be released within 3 to 5 business days depending on the issuing bank. Partial payments using a combination of Zepto Wallet balance and another payment method are supported at checkout. Zepto does not store full card details; only tokenised card references are retained for one-tap checkout. EMI (equated monthly instalment) options are available for eligible credit cards on orders above Rs. 3,000. Promotional credits and coupon discounts are applied before the final payment is charged and cannot be converted to cash. Customers experiencing payment issues can contact support through the app; duplicate charge disputes must be raised within 7 days of the transaction.
