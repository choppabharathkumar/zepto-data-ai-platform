Zepto Refund and Return Policy

Zepto accepts refund requests for damaged, expired, or incorrectly delivered items. Customers must raise a refund request within 48 hours of delivery through the app by navigating to Orders > Select Order > Report an Issue. Perishable items such as fruits, vegetables, dairy, and meat are eligible for refund only if reported within 12 hours of delivery with photographic evidence. Non-perishable items that are sealed and unopened may be returned within 7 days of delivery. Items sold under a flash sale, clearance, or promotional discount are non-refundable unless they arrive damaged. Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days; Zepto Wallet credits are applied within 24 hours. If a refund request is raised for a partial order, only the value of the affected items will be refunded, not the entire order. Zepto reserves the right to reject refund requests that do not comply with the policy or where abuse of the refund system is suspected. Customers who receive a refund as wallet credit may use it on their next purchase with no expiry for 90 days. For COD (cash on delivery) orders, refunds are issued exclusively as Zepto Wallet credits.
