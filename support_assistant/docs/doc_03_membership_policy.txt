Zepto Membership and ZeptoPass Policy

ZeptoPass is Zepto's premium subscription program that provides members with benefits including free delivery on all orders above Rs. 99, exclusive member-only discounts, and early access to flash sales. ZeptoPass is available in monthly and annual plans. The monthly plan is billed on the same date each month and auto-renews unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the renewal date. The annual plan is billed once per year and offers a discount of approximately 30% compared to the monthly rate. ZeptoPass benefits apply only to the account holding the subscription and cannot be transferred or shared. Cancellation of a ZeptoPass subscription takes effect at the end of the current billing period; no pro-rata refunds are provided for the unused portion. If a ZeptoPass subscription payment fails, the account will be downgraded to a standard account after a 48-hour grace period. Members can pause their subscription for up to 30 days once per billing year; delivery fee benefits are suspended during the pause. ZeptoPass does not cover delivery fees for orders that include restricted items such as alcohol in states where home delivery requires a premium delivery surcharge. Customer support for ZeptoPass billing issues is available through the app under Account > ZeptoPass > Help.
